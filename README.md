# AI-Powered SME Operations Hub

## Project Overview
The AI-Powered SME Operations Hub is a web-based business management platform designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises manage their daily operations from a centralized system.

The platform aims to simplify customer management, sales tracking, employee and task management, support requests, and business reporting. It also includes an AI-powered business assistant to provide useful insights from business data.

## Main Features
- User registration, login, and role-based access control
- Customer record management
- Sales management
- Employee and task management
- Customer support request management
- Business dashboard and reports
- AI-powered business insights
